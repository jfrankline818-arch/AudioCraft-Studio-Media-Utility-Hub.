Beats Per Minute

Understanding Musical Tempo & Digital Signal Rhythm

Beats per Minute (BPM) is the universal metric for measuring musical tempo. Accurate tempo tracking is vital for music production, podcast sound design, DJ track transitions, and audio-video synchronization.

Our client-side tap engine averages time intervals between user taps using millisecond-level Web API timestamps, allowing producers to quickly identify the cadence of any live recording or soundtrack.

About AudioCraft Studio

Acoustic utilities, digital audio tools, and publisher disclosures.

Our Mission

AudioCraft Studio provides musicians, sound engineers, podcasters, and media creators with browser-native acoustic tools. Our goal is to streamline everyday audio tasks like BPM calculation, tone generation, and bitrate estimation without forcing creators to download bulky software or upload audio files to external servers.

Client-Side Audio Security

We prioritize creator privacy. Audio synthesis and timing estimations are performed locally using HTML5 Web Audio APIs within your web browser. No recorded media, sound samples, or audio data streams are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.
